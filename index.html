Rooms & colours

Every activity happens in a room, and the room's colour on a printed agenda tells the participant where to go. The defaults below cover a normal assessment day — you can rename them or add more.

What do “= committee” and “shared” mean?

= committee — the room takes each committee's own colour automatically (used for the Exercise room, so each committee's exercises show in that committee's colour). Untick it to give the room one fixed colour instead.

shared — several participants may be in this room at the same time (like the Common room). Rooms that are not shared trigger a double-booking warning if two people are scheduled there at once.

Committees

A committee is a fixed pair of evaluators who assess their own group of participants for the whole day. The committee's colour marks its exercise room on every agenda.

✨ Let the app build the day beta

One click builds each committee's full day — introduction, exercise rotation, lunch and group exercise — with no double-bookings. You can drag things around afterwards, and “Check the schedule” tells you if anything breaks the rules.

Exercise library — what goes into a generated day

These are the building blocks the generator uses. prep = minutes a participant prepares alone in the common room; run = minutes in front of the committee. Untick use to leave an exercise out. order — leave on auto to let the generator stagger participants freely, or force an exercise to a fixed position (1st, 2nd…) in every participant's day. after — force an exercise to always come after another one. Durations are placeholders until the assessors confirm them.

Fine-tune by hand

Click an empty cell to add an activity, or drag across empty cells to set its length. Drag a coloured block to move it; drag its edge to make it longer or shorter.

Preview & print

Every agenda is automatically shrunk to fit exactly one A4 page, and the master grid prints sideways on its page — no printer settings to fiddle with. In the print window choose “Save as PDF” to get a file; if colours look missing, tick “Background graphics” there.
